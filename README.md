A one-image summary of Wednesday 25 November 2026: six flat-design story cards — the Houthis' second strike in five days on Riyadh's PIF Tower, Zelenskiy confirming Ukraine hit the Russian cargo ship Sparta II in the Gulf of Finland, the Ukrainian strike on the Russian corvette Merkury at Benghazi, tankers hit by mines in the Strait of Hormuz, Huawei's US criminal conviction, and England levelling the T20 series with Australia — plus an "also today" strip. The poster is a self-contained HTML page of inline SVG flat art, rendered to a 1024×1024 PNG, and the program publishes that PNG to the output directory.
